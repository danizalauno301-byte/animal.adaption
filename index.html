HOW IT WORKS

A Thoughtful Adoption Journey

Adoption is a commitment. Our process is simple, transparent, and focused on the wellbeing of both the animal and the adopter.

ANIMAL WELFARE

Responsible Adoption Starts with Care

A loving home is more than affection. It is patience, routine, proper nutrition, veterinary care, and a safe environment. We help adopters understand what each animal truly needs.

  • Health and vaccination information is recorded and reviewed before adoption.
  • Adopters are encouraged to choose an animal that matches their space and lifestyle.
  • Every application is reviewed with the animal's wellbeing as the priority.
  • Post-adoption guidance helps families support a smooth transition.

PET CARE NOTES

Small Habits, Better Lives

Simple practices that help every adopted animal feel safe, healthy, and loved.

🥣

Balanced nutrition

Feed age-appropriate food, keep fresh water available, and avoid sudden diet changes.

🩺

Routine veterinary care

Keep vaccinations current and schedule regular checkups, especially in the first weeks.

🧸

Patience and play

Give animals time to adjust, provide gentle exercise, and build trust through routine.

ABOUT PAWS & HEARTS

An Animal Welfare Platform, Not Just a Record System

The Animal Adoption and Care System brings adoption applications, animal profiles, medical records, and daily care into one organized platform for shelter staff and adopters.

It is built around a simple belief: every animal deserves a safe home, and every adoption deserves thoughtful preparation.

Clear animal profiles Photos, health details, and care information in one place.
Guided applications Adopters can apply and follow their application status.
Care monitoring Medical and daily care records stay organized for staff.
Responsible matching Reviews focus on the long-term wellbeing of the animal.
