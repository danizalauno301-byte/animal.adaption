ABOUT THE SYSTEM

Supporting Responsible Animal Adoption

The Animal Adoption and Care System is designed to help organize animal records, adoption applications, and medical care information.

The system provides a centralized platform where authorized personnel can manage animal information while prospective adopters can view available animals and submit applications.
